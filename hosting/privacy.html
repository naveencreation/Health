Nomzo Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Nomzo ("we", "the app") is a calorie, water, steps and weight tracker with an AI food-photo scanner and an AI coach. This page explains what the app collects, where it goes, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Short version: your logs live on your phone. If you create an account they are also backed up to our cloud so you can restore them. Photos and chat go to Google's Gemini AI only if you add your own Gemini key, and the key stays on your phone.

If you use the app as a guest (no account), your data stays on your phone only and is not uploaded to our cloud.

2. Accounts and cloud backup

If you sign up with an email and password, we use Firebase (a Google service) for sign-in, a database and photo storage. Each user can only read and write their own data. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.

3. AI food scanner and AI coach

These features are optional and work only when you add your own Google Gemini API key.

  • Your key is stored securely on your phone. It is never sent to our servers or saved in our database.
  • When you scan a meal or chat with the coach, the app sends the photo or your message, plus context such as your goals and today's totals, directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API using your key. We do not keep a copy on a server of ours.
  • Google handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy for the Gemini API. Depending on your Google plan, Google may use content you send as described in those terms. Please read them before adding a key.
  • AI results are estimates and can be wrong. Please check them before saving.

4. Other services the app talks to

  • Open Food Facts: when you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to look up the product. No account details are sent.
  • Android Health Connect (optional): if you allow it, the app reads your step count only. It does not write to Health Connect. You can revoke this in Health Connect settings.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to take or pick a meal photo.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your phone. They do not send your data anywhere. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. It is shared only with the service providers above (Google Firebase, and Google Gemini if you add a key) to run the features you use, or if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete it. Crash and usage data kept by Google Firebase is retained for a limited period under Google's own settings.

7. Your choices

  • Delete your account and data: in the app, go to Profile and choose Delete account. This removes your cloud profile, logs, meal photos and chat history, and clears data stored on the phone.
  • Edit or remove: you can edit or remove any entry in the app at any time.
  • Remove your AI key: delete it in the AI key settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Questions or requests about your data: email us at the address below.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Cloud access rules restrict each account to its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

9. Children

Nomzo is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 18, please use the app with a parent or guardian.

10. Not medical advice

Nomzo gives general nutrition information and estimates. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before major changes to your diet, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, or are under 18.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
