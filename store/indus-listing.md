## Long description

Meet Nomzo, the friendly flame who helps you eat well without the hard work.

📸 SNAP YOUR FOOD
Take a photo of your plate and Nomzo estimates the dish, portion, calories, protein, carbs and fat. Fix anything with a tap and log it in seconds.

🍛 MADE FOR INDIAN MEALS
Search a food catalogue full of everyday dishes, scan packaged foods by barcode, or save your own recipes as custom foods.

🔥 A PLAN BUILT FOR YOU
Answer a few quick questions and get a daily calorie and macro plan based on your body, activity and goal — lose, maintain or gain.

💬 ASK NOMZO
Chat with Nomzo, your AI nutrition coach. Ask what to eat for dinner, how to hit your protein, or why your weight stalled. Nomzo can log meals straight from the chat.

💧 WATER, 👟 STEPS, ⚖️ WEIGHT
Track water and other drinks, steps (sync with Health Connect on Android) and weight with BMI — all in one place.

📊 INSIGHTS THAT MAKE SENSE
See your week at a glance: days in your calorie zone, macro split, steps, water and weight trend, with a clear chart for each and Nomzo's tips on what to try next.

⏰ GENTLE REMINDERS
Optional reminders for meals, water, weigh-ins and steps, with quiet hours.

🔒 YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
Works offline. Sign in to back up and sync, or use it as a guest. Delete your account and data any time from the app.

All features are free during early access.

Good to know:
• Nomzo's AI features use Google Gemini with your own free Gemini API key, which you add once in the app. Your key stays on your phone.
• Nomzo gives general wellness guidance and is not a medical device or a substitute for advice from a doctor or dietitian. Talk to a professional if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or an eating disorder.
